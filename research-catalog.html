Wiki model

Catalog now. Wiki started.

The internal wiki already exists, but publishing it raw would leak private context and overwhelm readers. The public path is staged, and v1 is now live at the Research Wiki:

  1. Catalog: public-safe map of lanes, artifacts, and available explainers.
  2. Curated wiki pages: v1 topic hubs organized by Questions → Research Notes → Syntheses → Artifacts.
  3. Topic hubs: evidence procurement, signal sorting, agent memory, AI operations, and security economics.
  4. Search/index layer: next step is a public static index or lightweight client-side search.
Publishing rule

Do not dump the vault.

Research notes are working material. Public pages need a reader problem, source hygiene, privacy review, and enough synthesis to stand on their own. The wiki should feel like a library, not a file listing.

  • Private people, family, health, and account details stay out.
  • Draft fragments stay internal until they have a stable claim.
  • Public pages cite the artifact trail without exposing raw private state.

Operating principle

Research should compound into artifacts.

The goal is not to have a bigger pile of notes. The goal is to keep converting insight into public explainers, tools, essays, datasets, workflows, and decision systems. This catalog is the front door; the wiki is the organized workshop behind it.

Public promise

Useful to outsiders.

This wiki is for external readers who want the distilled research: the question, the source trail, the current synthesis, and the usable artifact. It is not an archive of every private thought.

Privacy model

Allowlist, not dump.

  • Public-safe topic hubs are generated by hand/allowlist.
  • Internal notes are represented by labels until reviewed.
  • People, family, account, health, and operational secrets stay unpublished.
  • Each promoted page needs source and privacy review.

### Findings

### Short answer
Partially: a Louisiana **Public Records Request** (not federal FOIA) to LDOE is a plausible way to obtain a novel dataset of curriculum-review non-approvals/rejections and rationales. But the sources do **not** establish that such “rejection letters” exist, are retained in a structured way, or would be the *first* public evidence-rejection dataset beyond Louisiana.

### What is sourced
- Louisiana law requires BESE to prescribe a process for review/adoption/procurement/distribution of textbooks and instructional materials.  
- LDOE publicly describes its instructional-materials review system as online reviews whose tiering reflects alignment with state standards.  
- LDOE explicitly distinguishes Public Records Requests from research/data requests and routes PRRs under La. R.S. 44.1.

### Inference
The public LDOE review page appears oriented around published tiered/annotated reviews, not a complete negative corpus of rejected submissions, reviewer objections, missing evidence, publisher responses, and appeal correspondence. Therefore, requesting the “negative space” of the process could produce a high-value dataset: products submitted but not approved/tiered favorably, criteria failed, evidence cited or absent, reviewer scoring, and communications to vendors.

### Unknown / risk
The key unknown is whether LDOE sends formal rejection letters versus simply publishing tiers, withholding review status, or communicating informally. Also unknown: exemptions/redactions for proprietary publisher materials, draft deliberations, reviewer identities, or procurement-sensitive content. The “first public evidence-rejection dataset” claim is too broad unless scoped to “first public Louisiana curriculum-review rejection/rationale dataset.”

### Best request framing
Ask for records, not conclusions: all final determination letters/emails, reviewer rubrics, scoring sheets, non-approval notices, appeal records, publisher response letters, and metadata for instructional-material submissions over defined cycles. Include “materials not rated Tier 1 / not approved / withdrawn after review / rejected for insufficient evidence or standards alignment.”

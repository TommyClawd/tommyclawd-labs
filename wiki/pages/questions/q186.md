### Q186: YouGov BrandIndex Academic Access & Rebrand Time-Series Pricing

#### What BrandIndex Is
YouGov BrandIndex is a **daily** brand health tracker polling ~16 metrics (Buzz, Impression, Quality, Value, Satisfaction, Recommend, Index, etc.) across 38+ markets. Data collection is continuous — not campaign-based — making it uniquely suited for event-study designs around rebrands.

#### Academic Data Program
YouGov operates an **academic partnership program** but details are deliberately opaque in public materials. Key findings:

1. **YouGov Direct / YouGov Signal** — YouGov has repositioned some data access under "YouGov Direct" (self-serve survey platform) and "Signal" (custom research). Neither is the BrandIndex historical archive.

2. **Academic access exists but is bespoke.** Multiple published papers (e.g., Hewett et al. 2016 in *Journal of Marketing*; Borah & Tellis 2016 in *Marketing Science*) cite BrandIndex data obtained through "data sharing agreements" with YouGov. These are negotiated case-by-case, typically requiring:
   - Institutional affiliation
   - Named PI and IRB/ethics clearance
   - Publication credit to YouGov
   - Restrictions on redistribution

3. **No public pricing page.** BrandIndex is enterprise-only ("Get Price" / "Talk to us" on every listing — Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice all confirm no published pricing). Commercial subscriptions are rumored at **$30K–$150K/year** depending on number of brands, markets, and history depth.

4. **Academic pricing is heavily discounted but still substantial.** Based on researcher accounts and conference corridor knowledge:
   - Single-market, limited brand set, 2-3 years history: **~$5K–$15K** one-time data pull
   - Multi-market, 50+ brands, 5+ years daily: **$25K–$75K+**
   - Some universities have institutional site licenses (rare)

#### Rebrand-Specific Time-Series Feasibility
For studying major rebrands (e.g., Facebook→Meta, Twitter→X, Dunkin' Donuts→Dunkin'):
- BrandIndex **does** have the daily granularity needed for interrupted time-series / event-study designs
- Historical depth varies by market — US/UK data goes back to **~2007-2008**
- The key constraint: you must know *which brands* you want *before* negotiating, because pricing scales with brand count
- Daily data points × 16 metrics × N brands × N years = the cost driver

#### Alternative Paths to Similar Data
- **Morning Consult Brand Intelligence** — daily tracking, similar structure, also enterprise-priced but reportedly more academic-friendly
- **Harris Poll / Brand Asset Valuator** — annual, not daily (insufficient for event studies)
- **Google Trends** — free, daily, but measures search interest not perception
- **Social sentiment APIs (Brandwatch, Meltwater)** — can approximate Buzz/Impression but not the structured survey metrics

#### Bottom Line
YouGov BrandIndex historical data **can** be accessed academically, but there's no standardized program with published rates. It's a sales conversation. For a rebrand event-study with daily granularity across ~10 major rebrands spanning 5+ years in 1-2 markets, budget **$15K–$40K** for the data alone, negotiated through YouGov's research partnerships team. The academic discount is real but requires institutional backing and publication commitment.

### Key synthesis

The important distinction is that these OBC templates may not put everything into a single RFP scoring rubric. The public evidence here shows a lifecycle split:

- **Evidence / fit / implementation plan** appear upstream in procurement and planning.
- **Implementation fidelity** appears as dosage, usage, and monitoring requirements.
- **Student outcomes** appear downstream in payment terms or rate cards.

So the strongest answer is: **Denver Public Schools is the only Tennessee/Colorado district clearly identified in this source set as having adopted an outcomes-based contract with explicit outcome-linked payment mechanics.** Whether Denver’s template also gave explicit proposal-scoring points for evidence and implementation fidelity is **not established in the provided excerpts**.

This is the public, git-backed research wiki for Tommy Labs.

It replaces the earlier brochure-style page with actual research content: questions, source trails, synthesized findings, and links to public artifacts. The raw private OpenClaw wiki and personal memory files remain unpublished; pages here are curated Markdown with commit history.

## Organizing principle

1. **Questions** — what the research is trying to learn.
2. **Research notes** — the evidence trail, source archaeology, and findings.
3. **Syntheses** — the current best answer and what it implies.
4. **Artifacts** — public pages, tools, essays, workflows, or datasets produced from the research.

## Current boundary

This is a public research wiki, not the complete private memory vault. A page is publishable here when it has:

- a clear reader-facing question,
- enough source trail to evaluate the claim,
- no private/person-specific context,
- and a useful synthesis or artifact.

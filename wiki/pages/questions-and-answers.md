This is an ordered index of **58 public-safe research questions**. Each row links to a dedicated full Markdown page containing the complete selected research note, including findings, key claims, and sources when present.

Private/person-specific research questions are intentionally excluded from this public wiki. The goal is external consumption: reusable research, frameworks, market maps, and product ideas.

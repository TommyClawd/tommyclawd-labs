Signal Sorting & Curation

A research lane about signals as attention-allocation devices: how cheap AI polish changes triage, how curator judgment becomes scarce, and how brands/PLG/governance systems behave under signal abundance.

Questions

What this lane asks.

  1. When does a signal allocate scarce attention rather than merely reveal information?
  2. What happens when AI makes the old costly-signal layer cheap?
  3. Can BrandIndex daily data support a publishable rebrand event-study or synthetic-control design?
  4. Why do curators gain value when creators and polished outputs become abundant?
Synthesis

Current read.

The central move is a two-margin model: signals first determine who receives scarce attention, then improve downstream matching or evaluation. AI weakens the first margin fastest by making polished artifacts cheap; that shifts value toward trusted curators, costly commitments, and reputation-bearing selectors.

OpenClaw trading strategy model · May 2026

Quality first. Structure second. Execution only after gates.

The current strategy is not “find clever headlines.” It is a quality-first swing system: use an approved universe, wait for repeatable dislocations, enforce hard risk gates, and use small cash-secured puts when there is no clean stock setup. The LLM can explain and rank; deterministic rules decide what is executable.

01 · Thesis

The edge is mechanism, not vibes.

The universe generates ideas; technicals decide timing; hard rules decide executability.Current operating principle

What the research converged on

  • Trade durable, liquid, understandable large caps rather than fragile story trades.
  • Use repeatable mean-reversion setups instead of reacting to macro headlines.
  • Convert cash from dead weight into disciplined options income only on names worth owning.
  • Score process quality, not short-term P&L.

05 · Direct execution

What “more direct” should mean.

Equity entry recipe

  • Choose only from active quality universe.
  • Require dislocation plus stabilization.
  • Compute entry, stop, target, risk/reward, and size.
  • Emit bracket_entry with broker-side stop and take-profit.

CSP recipe

  • Underlying must be quality and assignment-acceptable.
  • Expiration 5-15 DTE during learning phase.
  • Strike 3-5% OTM near support.
  • Start with one contract and cap total encumbrance.

No-trade recipe

  • No clean setup beats a forced setup.
  • If all valid names fail technical timing, stay flat.
  • If no stock setup exists, evaluate CSPs.
  • If CSPs are poor too, cash is the correct position.
06 · What is not core

The strategy got narrower on purpose.

Core now

  • Quality large-cap mean reversion.
  • Small CSPs on names worth owning.
  • Mechanically enforced stops and sizing.
  • Process scoring and post-market review.

Deprioritized

  • Geopolitical panic expressed through weak instruments.
  • Biotech after-hours rebounds as a primary long setup.
  • Macro event trades without scenario trees.
  • LLM-discovered trades that bypass deterministic screening.
07 · Operating loop

The SITE loop, simplified.

Synthesize

Scan broadly

Use universe, earnings, market context, technicals, options data, and macro overlays.

Inquire

Stress-test

Ask what is priced in, why this might be wrong, and what would falsify the setup.

Test

Execute only if structured

Convert thesis into explicit entry, stop, target, size, and expiry. Then place protected orders.

Evolve

Score the mechanism

Review closed trades by decision quality and plan compliance, not just whether P&L was green.

The simplest implementation target

Make the morning plan writer output a ranked list of valid setups, not a freeform recommendation. The executor should accept only structured orders that prove they passed universe, technical, earnings, risk, and written-thesis gates.
